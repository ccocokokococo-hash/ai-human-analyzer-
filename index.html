Theoretical Part

AI Writing vs Human Writing

AI and human texts can both be useful, but they sound different. Students can compare them using clear criteria.

🤖

AI Writing

  • Usually correct grammar
  • Formal vocabulary
  • Very clear structure
  • General examples
  • Less personal emotion
VS
🧑‍🎓

Human Writing

  • May have small mistakes
  • Simple and natural words
  • Personal examples
  • Real emotions
  • Individual writing style

Example

Human

I like drawing because it makes me calm. Sometimes I draw after school when I feel tired.

AI

Drawing is an enjoyable activity that helps people relax and express their creativity.

Conclusion

AI writing and human writing have clear differences. AI texts are usually correct and formal, while human texts are more personal and natural. This product helps students compare texts, explain their answers and develop critical thinking.

Recommendations

Do not copy AI texts. Use AI for ideas and structure. Add personal examples. Keep your own writing style.
